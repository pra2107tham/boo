Why this reads like the references
Atmosphere behind glass
Three blurred blobs at 34px blur, then a translucent dark layer over the top. Same recipe as the orange bloom on Rightfit — soft colour that never competes with the text.
The bloom is the state
Green when calm, hot orange when strained, teal and violet with music, dim indigo asleep. You read the mood before you read a single word.
Numbers, not rows
Three big figures side by side instead of four stacked rows with bars. Roughly 40% shorter, and the panel stops looking like a settings screen.
