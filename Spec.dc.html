Motion
WhatTiming
Idle float±0.5 px vertical, 3 s ease-in-out, forever
Blink120 ms close-open, random every 4–7 s
Glanceeyes shift 2 px, hold 900 ms, return. ~1 in 4 blinks
Heartbeatscale 1.0→1.15, interval maps CPU: 4 s idle → 0.4 s pegged
Hem ripplewaves offset 80 ms apart, only while floating
State change250 ms cross-fade, eyes morph, no bounce
Headphone bob±1 px, 500 ms, only when audio is actually playing
All animation pauses when the display sleeps or on battery saver. A menu bar pet that drains your battery is a bad joke.

Geometry
64×64 grid. Body spans x 12–55, y 5–52.
Eyes on y 31 — just below centre. That is the baby proportion; moving them up ages the character instantly.
Eye centres x 23 and 41, radius 4.3×4.6 — very slightly taller than wide.
Heart is a 3 px pixel grid so it stays crisp. Hem is three waves, never four.
Scope — v1 ships this and nothing more
IN Six states · four panel rows · blink and gaze · heartbeat · light and dark · launch at login toggle
OUT Graphs · history · per-core detail · process list · notifications · costume days · themes · preferences window beyond one small panel
Costume days and extra moods are easy to add once the shape ships. Adding them now is how this turns into a six-month project that never launches.
