Why it thinks music is always on
Diagnosed on your machine, with music stopped. The flag currently being used means something different from what the name suggests.
THE BUG
RunningSomewhere(global) = 1
RunningSomewhere(output) = 1
IsRunning(global)        = 0
audio processes = 60
actively OUTPUTTING = 1
kAudioDevicePropertyDeviceIsRunningSomewhere means “a stream is open on this device”, not “sound is coming out”. Any app holding the device pins it to 1 forever — a muted browser tab, an idle Zoom, a paused player. It essentially never returns to 0 on a working Mac.
THE FIX
kAudioHardwarePropertyProcessObjectList
  → for each process
     kAudioProcessPropertyIsRunningOutput
Ask per process whether it is actively pushing output. Verified on your Mac: 1 of 60 processes was genuinely outputting while the device flag claimed everything was playing. Public API, macOS 14.4+, no permissions.

The pause between tracks is shorter than the grace period, so Boo never stutters mid-playlist. Silence has to hold a full 3 s before it counts as stopped — the same hysteresis idea that keeps the CPU face from flickering during a build.
Full decision order
1Is an output device present? If not, hide the row entirely.
2Is it muted, or is volume zero? Then it is silent, whatever else says.
3Is any process actively outputting? That is the real signal.
4Silent for under 3 s? Keep the previous answer.
5On older macOS, fall back to the device flag plus volume.
Each of these becomes an assertion in --self-check, including the paused-but-open case that is broken today.
