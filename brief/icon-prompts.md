## Light-background check

Once you have 2–3 favourites, re-run those same prompts with one word changed —
swap `plain dark charcoal #1c1c1e background` for `plain light warm grey #f2f0ec
background`. The menu bar is dark in dark mode and light otherwise, so the mark
has to hold in both.

---

## Judging

Two questions only:

1. Shrink it to thumbnail size and squint — is the silhouette still distinct?
2. Look away for 5 seconds — could you draw it from memory?

Anything that fails either one is out, however pretty it is.

## What each is testing

- **1 Ghost** — wavy bottom edge is a distinctive silhouette; no mouth forces gaze to do the work
- **2 Squircle** — the Clyde geometry, proven at favicon size; the bumps are the memorable part
- **3 Orb** — pure circle, gaze does everything; highest risk of reading as a generic emoji
- **4 Cat** — ear notches read instantly at tiny size; most conventionally cute
- **5 Bean** — only non-round silhouette; tests whether calm can be the resting state
- **6 Robot** — visor reads as one strong band at 18px; most tool, least pet
- **7 Dumpling** — the top pinch is a cheap distinctive detail; very huggable
- **8 Moth** — widest silhouette, stands out in a row of square icons; antennae may vanish at 18px
