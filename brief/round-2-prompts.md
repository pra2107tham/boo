# Round 2 — ghost × dumpling, cuter, with a detail

The two winners crossed with each other, plus the retouches. Copy a block, paste,
run 4x. Nothing to append.

What changed from round 1, and why:

- **Eyes lower and wider apart.** High, close eyes read as adult. Low and wide
  is the baby-face proportion — this is the single biggest cuteness lever.
- **Bigger eyes.** Round 1 dots were too small to hold at 18px anyway.
- **Wider than tall.** Squat reads friendlier than upright.
- **One asymmetric detail.** Perfect symmetry is what made round 1 feel like
  clip art. A single off-centre element gives it a character.
- **Blush.** Cheap, universally reads as cute, survives small sizes as two dots.

On the logo idea: an Apple logo can't ship on an open-source app — it's
trademarked. Same for any existing brand mark. So the "little detail" is
something of our own: a bite taken out, a sprout, a cowlick, a pixel heart.
Prompt 5 does the tech-detail version properly.

## Judging

Same two questions, plus one new:

1. Shrink to thumbnail, squint — silhouette still distinct?
2. Look away 5 seconds — could you draw it from memory?
3. Does the extra detail survive shrinking, or does it turn to mush?

If the detail turns to mush it can still live in the popover at large size and
be dropped from the menu bar version — that is a normal thing to do, not a
failure.
